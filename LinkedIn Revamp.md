## Using your 500+ connections
1. Sort them (2 hours, one time): current/past colleagues, college friends, seniors at product companies, recruiters, people in cinema/media-tech. Aim for a list of about 30 people who could actually help.
2. Update the profile first. Every message you send makes people click through to it.
3. Start posting (the 90-day plan). Reply to each comment. Comment on 5 posts a day from your connections, with a real thought and not just "Great post!".
4. Ask for recommendations. 2–3 short written recommendations from seniors or teammates beat any headline. Make it easy: send them 3 bullet points to remind them what you worked on.
5. Ask for referrals the right way. Don't write "please refer me". Write a short message (below) with the specific job link and your CV attached.
6. Use 2nd-degree connections. Search a target company, filter by "Connections: 2nd", and ask a mutual connection for an intro.
7. Don't mass-message. Ten personal messages a week beats a hundred copied ones.
