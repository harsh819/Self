A bug lived in production for 3–4 years.

A cinema client's box-office and auditorium displays would not show shows scheduled after 00:00.

Everyone knew about it. Nobody had fixed it. It had become "just how the system works".

I picked it up, and it was closed in 2 weeks.

The fix wasn't clever. What changed was how I approached it:
1. I stopped trusting the explanations people had already given.
2. I went to the data instead of the code.
3. I made the evidence impossible to argue with.

Old bugs survive because every new person assumes someone else already looked properly.
