What 3 years of watching production systems taught me:

1. Recurring issues are never "just recurring". There is a cause nobody has written down yet.
2. The loudest symptom is rarely the root cause. The error is often three layers away from the problem.
3. "It works on my machine" means the environments differ, so find the difference.
4. Reporting clearly is half of fixing. A clear write-up gets a fix scheduled faster than a long argument.
5. Every new launch is a chance to prevent next month's incident. Test locally and properly before it goes live.

I thought production support would slow my growth as a developer. It did the opposite: I learned how real systems fail.
