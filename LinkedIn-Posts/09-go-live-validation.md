The best time to fix a production issue is before it's in production.

Whenever a new client site goes live, I test everything locally first:

→ Does the setup match what the client will actually use?
→ Do the edge cases behave? (late-night times, unusual configs, missing data)
→ Can I reproduce a problem if one appears later?
