## Before you post anything
- **[BRACKETS]** mark facts only you know (numbers, specifics). Fill them in or delete the line. Don't post a bracket.
- **Never name the client, cinema, city or show data.** Say "a cinema client". Your employment agreement has a confidentiality clause.
- **Check with your senior/manager** before posting posts 02, 04 and 05 (they describe client work). A quick "I'll describe it anonymously, ok?" avoids trouble.
- I skipped a post about using an AI tool on the client's DB access. Posting that could look like client data went into a third-party tool. Only write it if your company approves it.
- Posts are drafts in a plain voice. Change words so it sounds like you.
